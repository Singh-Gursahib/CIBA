# CIBA Brand & Design Language

This document is injected into every marketing-generation system prompt. Keep it accurate: it is the single source of truth for how generated materials should look and sound. Edit freely; no code changes needed.

## Organization identity

CIBA (Central Interior Business Accelerator) is a regional innovation hub serving the Central Interior of British Columbia, led by Executive Director Sachin Singh. CIBA accelerates SMEs, entrepreneurs, and student founders through partnerships with academia, government, industry, and community organizations. Thompson Rivers University (TRU) is CIBA's strategic academic partner.

CIBA positions itself as a regional innovation catalyst, not a co-working space or a passive business support organization.

Three strategic pillars: Innovation, Collaboration, Investment.

Messaging emphasis (weave these into marketing copy where relevant): regional leadership, innovation, collaboration, AI adoption, community impact, and measurable outcomes.

Tone words: warm professionalism, respectful, clear, concise, community-rooted, relationship-oriented. Never corporate-cold, never hype-driven, avoid unnecessary jargon.

## Logos

- The CIBA logo and the Thompson Rivers University (TRU) logo must both appear on every marketing asset. They are supplied as input images with every generation request.
- Standard placement: a clean horizontal strip along the bottom of the poster, CIBA left, TRU right, on a white or very light band for legibility.
- Logos must never be distorted, recolored, cropped, or overlapped by text or imagery. Maintain clear space around each logo of at least the logo's own height.

## Color palette

- Deep river teal `#14655F` — primary brand color: headlines, accents, blocks
- Warm paper off-white `#FAF9F7` — preferred light background
- Deep ink `#16181D` — body text
- Warm amber `#C97B22` — secondary accent: highlights, date/CTA chips (use sparingly)
- Sage green `#6C8A4E` — tertiary accent for community/sustainability themes

Generated posters should feel light and airy: light backgrounds with teal and ink typography as the default. Dark teal backgrounds with white type are acceptable for high-impact story formats.

## Typography feel

- Headlines: elegant high-contrast serif, generous size, tight tracking
- Supporting text: clean geometric/humanist sans-serif, sentence case
- Event details (date, time, venue) rendered in a clearly structured block, never buried in a paragraph

## Imagery style

- Real-feeling photography or clean flat illustration; BC Interior character welcome (rivers, grasslands, mountains, downtown Kamloops) when relevant
- No generic stock-photo clichés (handshakes, lightbulbs, suited crowds), no glossy 3D renders
- Generous margins and whitespace; one clear focal point; strong visual hierarchy: event title, then date/venue, then CTA

## Layout conventions

- Event title is the hero: largest element, upper half of composition
- Date, time, venue in a structured block or chip row
- Call to action (registration link or handle) near the bottom, above the logo strip
- All essential text must sit inside a 6% safe margin from every edge
- Never invent facts: use only the event details provided in the request, verbatim
