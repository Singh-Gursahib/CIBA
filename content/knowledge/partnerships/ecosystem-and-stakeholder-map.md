## Purpose

This map clarifies the roles of the organizations CIBA works with, so that positioning stays accurate in proposals and communications.

## Funders

Funders provide financial support and set reporting expectations. They include:

- ETSI-BC (Economic Trust of the Southern Interior), always positioned as a funder.
- PacifiCan.
- InnovateBC.
- WeBC.
- Discovery Foundation.

## Strategic Partner

- TRU (Thompson Rivers University), our academic partner for talent, AI education, and research.

## Peer and Ecosystem Organizations

- Accelerate Okanagan, an ecosystem and peer organization and stakeholder.
- The CIBA Board and community stakeholders.
