## Overview

Thompson Rivers University (TRU) is CIBA's strategic academic partner. The partnership focuses on talent development, AI education, entrepreneurship, hackathons, research collaboration, and student innovation across the Central Interior.

## Scope of Collaboration

- AI education and workshops for students.
- Startup training and innovation challenges.
- Hackathons and interdisciplinary micro-teams.
- Research collaboration and career pathways.

## Why It Matters

TRU is central to our pillar of Collaboration and to the roadmap priority of deepening TRU collaboration. Student talent is a core input to the Innovation Flywheel: graduates and student founders feed directly into SME growth across the region.

## Governance

The relationship is formalized through a memorandum of understanding (MOU) that clarifies roles, shared goals, and reporting expectations, consistent with our approach to sustainability.

## Activity Snapshot

| Activity | Cadence |
|----------|---------|
| AI workshops | Semester based |
| Hackathons | Twice per year |
