## Overview

Local Links strengthens regional supply chains by connecting local suppliers with local buyers. The initiative reduces leakage from the regional economy and supports our theme of Economic Impact.

## Core Activities

- Supplier and buyer matchmaking.
- AI-assisted matching to surface strong fits.
- Collaboration events that bring partners together.
- Local procurement tracking to measure results.

## How It Works

CIBA maintains a supplier database and runs matchmaking events where buyers meet vetted local suppliers. Matches are then tracked so that local procurement outcomes can be reported to funders and stakeholders.

## Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Suppliers in database | 100 or more |
| Matches facilitated | 40 per year |
| Annual supply chain event | One |

## Sustainability

An annual supply chain event and ongoing supplier database maintenance anchor the initiative's long-term sustainability.
