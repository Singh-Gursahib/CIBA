## Overview

This project helps regional SMEs adopt clean technologies and sustainable practices. It focuses on AI-enabled resource efficiency, waste reduction, sustainable development, and innovation in clean technologies.

## Focus Areas

- AI-enabled resource efficiency for energy, materials, and water.
- Waste reduction across operations.
- Sustainable development aligned with regional priorities.
- Innovation in clean technologies.

## Approach

CIBA works with SMEs to identify practical efficiency opportunities, pilot solutions, and measure environmental and economic results. This links sustainability directly to our pillar of Innovation.

## Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| SMEs engaged | 10 or more |
| Waste reduction reported | 10 percent average |
| Clean-tech pilots | 5 or more |
