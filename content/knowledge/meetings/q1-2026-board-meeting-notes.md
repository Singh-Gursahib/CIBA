## Meeting Details

- **Date:** February 15, 2026.
- **Chair:** Executive Director Sachin Singh with the CIBA Board.
- **Focus:** funding status, program progress, and sustainability planning.

## Funding Update

The Board reviewed the funding pipeline. PacifiCan and ETSI-BC support is confirmed and in delivery, while InnovateBC, Discovery Foundation, and WeBC proposals are submitted and awaiting decisions.

## Program Progress

- The [[SME AI Pilot Program]] is on track for a cohort of 12 to 15 companies.
- The [[Core Workshop Series]] completed its first two workshops with strong attendance.
- [[Student Innovation Programming]] confirmed hackathon dates with TRU.

## Decisions

| Item | Decision |
|------|----------|
| Impact Forum 2026 | Approved for spring delivery |
| Mentor expansion | Approved for the next quarter |

## Sustainability Discussion

The Board discussed quarterly peer circles, supplier database maintenance, and future training revenue as pillars of long-term sustainability.

## Related Documents

The Board reaffirmed alignment with the [[Strategic Plan 2025 to 2027]] and governance in [[Team and Governance]]. It approved planning for the [[CIBA Impact Forum 2026]] and reviewed funder status across the pipeline.
