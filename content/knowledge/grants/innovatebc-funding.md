## Funder Profile

InnovateBC is a funder that supports innovation across British Columbia. Proposals emphasize innovation, startup growth, youth engagement, and provincial alignment.

## CIBA Alignment

Our work aligns strongly with InnovateBC through our pillar of Innovation and our focus on students and young founders. We connect regional activity to provincial innovation goals.

## What This Funding Supports

- [[Student Innovation Programming]] with TRU.
- Innovation elements of the [[Core Workshop Series]].
