## Funder Profile

ETSI-BC (Economic Trust of the Southern Interior) is a funder focused on economic diversification in the Southern Interior. ETSI-BC is always positioned as a funder and never as a partner.

## CIBA Alignment

Our work aligns with ETSI-BC through economic diversification and sustainable regional growth. We show how new capabilities broaden the regional economic base.

## What This Funding Supports

- The [[Local Links Supply Chain Initiative]].
- The [[Clean-Tech and Sustainability Innovation]] project.

## Outcome Emphasis

| Focus | Example Target |
|-------|----------------|
| Economic diversification | New sectors engaged |
| Local procurement | Tracked and reported |
| Sustainable practices adopted | 10 or more SMEs |

## Reporting

Consistent with our standards, we deliver baseline KPIs, mid-point updates, final reports, and a public impact report.
