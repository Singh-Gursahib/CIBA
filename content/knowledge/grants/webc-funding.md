## Funder Profile

WeBC is a funder that supports women entrepreneurs. Proposals emphasize women entrepreneurs, mentorship, inclusivity, and community support.

## CIBA Alignment

Inclusivity is a core part of our programming philosophy. We show how CIBA expands access and support for women-led businesses across the Central Interior.

## What This Funding Supports

- The [[CIBA Mentorship Program]], including dedicated mentorship for women entrepreneurs.
- Inclusive participation in the [[Core Workshop Series]].
