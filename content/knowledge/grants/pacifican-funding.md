## Funder Profile

PacifiCan is a funder that supports economic development across British Columbia. Proposals to PacifiCan emphasize measurable economic impact, quantified KPIs, realistic budgets, evidence-based planning, and strong logic models.

## CIBA Alignment

Our programming aligns with PacifiCan through its focus on Economic Impact and disciplined delivery. We present clear logic models linking activities to regional outcomes.

## What This Funding Supports

- The [[SME AI Pilot Program]] and its metrics tracking.
- Elements of the [[Local Links Supply Chain Initiative]].

## KPI Emphasis

| KPI | Example Target |
|-----|----------------|
| SMEs supported | 12 to 15 |
| Jobs supported or created | 20 or more |
| Local procurement value tracked | Reported annually |

## Reporting

We provide baseline KPIs, mid-point updates, and final reports, followed by a public impact report.
