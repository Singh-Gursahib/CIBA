## Purpose

This plan sets CIBA's direction from 2025 to 2027 as we build a sustainable regional innovation ecosystem for the Central Interior of British Columbia.

## Three Strategic Pillars

- **Innovation:** advancing AI adoption and new capabilities across the region.
- **Collaboration:** connecting SMEs, students, funders, and partners.
- **Investment:** securing funder support and driving reinvestment.

## Roadmap Priorities

1. AI adoption across SMEs.
2. Deepening TRU collaboration.
3. Expanding SME innovation capacity.
4. Strong funder programming.
5. A sustainable regional innovation ecosystem.

## Five Strategic Themes

| Theme | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| Innovation Enablement | New tools and capabilities for SMEs |
| Collaboration | Shared ecosystem and partnerships |
| Economic Impact | Measurable regional outcomes |
| AI Capacity Building | Practical AI skills and pilots |
| Operational Excellence | Disciplined delivery and reporting |

## Long-Term Vision

Our vision is an Innovation Flywheel, where AI, innovation, collaboration, student talent, SME growth, and funder reinvestment reinforce one another, creating momentum that compounds over time.
