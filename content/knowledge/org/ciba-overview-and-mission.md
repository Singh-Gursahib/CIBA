## Who We Are

The Central Interior Business Accelerator (CIBA) is a regional innovation hub serving the Central Interior of British Columbia. Operating from acceleratebusiness.ca, CIBA accelerates SMEs, entrepreneurs, and student founders through partnerships with academia, government, industry, and community organizations.

CIBA positions itself as a regional innovation catalyst, not a co-working space or a passive business support organization. Every initiative is designed to create measurable outcomes for the region.

## Mission

Our mission is to strengthen the regional economy by enabling innovation, deepening collaboration, and attracting investment across the Central Interior.

## Three Strategic Pillars

1. **Innovation:** helping businesses adopt new tools, including AI, to grow and compete.
2. **Collaboration:** connecting SMEs, students, funders, and partners into a shared ecosystem.
3. **Investment:** channeling funder support and reinvestment into sustainable regional capacity.

## What We Do

- Deliver practical, action-oriented programming for SMEs and founders.
- Partner deeply with academia to develop student talent.
- Build a sustainable regional innovation ecosystem.

Our long-term vision is an Innovation Flywheel, where AI, innovation, collaboration, student talent, SME growth, and funder reinvestment reinforce one another.
