## Executive Leadership

CIBA is led by Executive Director Sachin Singh, who is responsible for strategy, funder relationships, and organizational performance. The Executive Director works closely with the Board to ensure that programming aligns with regional priorities and delivers measurable impact.

## Board of Directors

The CIBA Board provides governance, financial oversight, and strategic guidance. The Board sets priorities in partnership with community stakeholders and reviews progress against the organization's goals throughout the year.

## Governance Responsibilities

| Function | Owner |
|----------|-------|
| Strategy and direction | Executive Director and Board |
| Financial oversight | Board |
| Program delivery | Executive Director and staff |
| Stakeholder relationships | Executive Director |

## Operating Approach

Governance at CIBA reflects our theme of Operational Excellence. We maintain clear reporting, disciplined budgets, and transparent decision-making. Memoranda of understanding (MOUs) formalize key relationships and support long-term sustainability.

## Community Stakeholders

CIBA engages community stakeholders across the Central Interior to keep programming grounded in local needs. These relationships anchor our credibility as a regional innovation catalyst.
