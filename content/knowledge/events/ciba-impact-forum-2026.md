## Overview

The CIBA Impact Forum 2026 is a knowledge-sharing event that brings funders, SMEs, students, and community stakeholders together. It features funders, success stories, showcases, lessons learned, and sustainability planning.

## Agenda Highlights

- Opening on regional innovation and the three pillars: Innovation, Collaboration, Investment.
- Success stories from SME AI pilots.
- Student showcase from TRU micro-teams.
- Funder panel and sustainability planning session.

The Forum celebrates measurable community impact, shares lessons learned, and strengthens relationships that sustain the regional innovation ecosystem.
