## Overview

The Core Workshop Series delivers four practical, action-oriented workshops that build innovation and AI capacity for SMEs across the Central Interior. The series reflects our programming philosophy: practical learning, measurable impact, and repeatable frameworks.

## The Four Workshops

| # | Workshop |
|---|----------|
| 1 | IP Strategy for Canadian Businesses |
| 2 | AI for Operational Efficiency & Growth |
| 3 | AI-Enhanced Supply Chain & Local Market Integration |
| 4 | Building a Sustainable Innovation Mindset |

## Format

Each workshop runs as a half-day session with a target cohort of 20 to 25 participants. Sessions combine short teaching, worked examples, and an action plan that participants complete before the next workshop.

## Outcomes

- Participants leave with a concrete next step for AI adoption.
- Businesses connect practical tools to growth and efficiency.
- The series feeds directly into deeper engagements.

## Related Documents

The series supports the [[Strategic Plan 2025 to 2027]] and prepares businesses for the [[SME AI Pilot Program]]. Workshop 3 connects to the [[Local Links Supply Chain Initiative]]. Delivery is supported through [[Discovery Foundation Funding]] and [[InnovateBC Funding]].
