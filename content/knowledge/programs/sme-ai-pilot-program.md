## Overview

The SME AI Pilot Program supports approximately 12 to 15 companies through a full AI adoption cycle. It is CIBA's flagship vehicle for the roadmap priority of AI adoption across SMEs.

## Program Stages

1. **Readiness assessment:** evaluate each company's data, processes, and goals.
2. **Pilot implementation:** deploy a focused AI use case.
3. **Metrics tracking:** measure results against a baseline.
4. **Reporting:** document outcomes and lessons learned.

## Cohort Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Companies supported | 12 to 15 |
| Pilots completed | 12 or more |
| Efficiency gain reported | 15 percent average |

## Measurement

Each pilot begins with baseline KPIs, receives a mid-point update, and ends with a final report. Aggregate results feed public impact reporting, reinforcing our theme of Economic Impact.
