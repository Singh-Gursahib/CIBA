## Overview

Student Innovation Programming is delivered in collaboration with TRU to develop the next generation of founders and innovators in the Central Interior. Students are a core input to the Innovation Flywheel.

## Program Elements

- AI workshops and startup training.
- Hackathons and innovation challenges.
- Interdisciplinary micro-teams.
- Personal branding and mentorship.
- Career pathways into regional SMEs.

## Delivery Model

Programming runs on a semester rhythm, with hackathons twice per year and an annual innovation challenge. Micro-teams of four to six students tackle real problems sourced from regional businesses.

## Impact Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Students engaged per year | 150 or more |
| Micro-teams formed | 20 or more |
| Student founders supported | 10 or more |
