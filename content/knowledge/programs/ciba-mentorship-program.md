## Overview

The CIBA Mentorship Program pairs regional SMEs with experienced mentors to accelerate growth. The program uses mentor playbooks, scoring rubrics, and structured action plans to keep engagements consistent and outcome-focused.

## Focus Areas

- AI adoption tracking.
- IP strategy.
- Business operations.
- Finance.
- Technology implementation.

## How It Works

Each business begins with a scored assessment. Mentors then work through a playbook and set an action plan with clear milestones. Progress is tracked against the rubric so that impact stays measurable, consistent with our theme of Operational Excellence.

## Rubric Snapshot

| Dimension | Scored |
|-----------|--------|
| AI adoption readiness | Yes |
| Operations maturity | Yes |
| Financial health | Yes |

## Sustainability

Mentor expansion is a core part of our sustainability planning, alongside quarterly peer circles that keep mentors and businesses connected between engagements.
