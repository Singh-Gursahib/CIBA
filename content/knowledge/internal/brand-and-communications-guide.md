## Brand Positioning

CIBA is a regional innovation catalyst for the Central Interior of British Columbia, never a co-working space or a passive business support organization. Messaging emphasizes regional leadership, innovation, collaboration, AI adoption, community impact, and measurable outcomes.

## Three Pillars in Messaging

Always reflect the three pillars: Innovation, Collaboration, Investment. Use them to frame stories and proposals.

## Voice and Style

Our tone is warm professionalism: respectful, clear, concise, relationship-oriented, and stakeholder-aware. Avoid unnecessary jargon. Preserve intent while improving clarity.

## Email Framework

| Step | Element |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Greeting |
| 2 | Warm opening |
| 3 | Purpose |
| 4 | Brief explanation |
| 5 | Request |
| 6 | Optional flexibility |
| 7 | Warm closing |

## Writing Rules

- Keep sentences clear and readable.
- Lead with the reader's interest.
- Ground claims in measurable outcomes.
